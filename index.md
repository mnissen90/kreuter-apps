# Kreuter Apps

Practical applications for better business workflows.

Kreuter Apps is operated by V5 Joining Technologies.

## Project Budget Guard

Project Budget Guard helps Jira teams monitor project labor costs against a configured budget and forecast expected final labor cost using Jira worklogs.

## Margin Leak

Margin Leak shows Shopify merchants exactly how much profit their discounts cost — comparing profit before and after discounts per product and order, and flagging anything below a target gross margin.

## Margin Guard

Margin Guard is a Shopify POS tile that shows staff the cart's live gross margin and the maximum safe discount — blocking further discounting once the margin floor is reached.
